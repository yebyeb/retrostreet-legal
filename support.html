Frequently Asked Questions

What games are included?

All 12 games are included from the start: Dodgeball, Marbles, Tug of War, Penalty Shootout, Air Hockey, Basketball, Spinning Top Arena, Taso (cap flip), Snowball Raid, Water Balloon, Tin-Can Knockdown and Road Crossing.

Is the game free?

Yes. The game is free to play and supported by ads. An optional one-time purchase removes all ads permanently.

How do I remove the ads?

Tap the “Remove Ads” button on the main menu and complete the purchase. If you have already bought it and reinstalled the app, sign in with the same Apple ID — there is no extra charge.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All 12 games can be played offline in single player and in 2-player mode on the same phone. An internet connection is required only for online multiplayer matches and to display ads.

How does online multiplayer work?

Choose a game, then create a room or join a friend's room with a code. Both players race through the same level and the first to finish wins. If you cannot connect, check your internet connection and make sure both players use the latest version.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Several games support 2 players on the same device — pick “2 Players on the Same Phone” in the lobby.

Do the levels ever end?

No. Each game has handcrafted levels that keep cycling with increasing difficulty and changing environments, so you can keep playing as long as you like.

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes your stars and level progress. Reinstalling the app does not restore it.

The game is not working properly. What should I do?

Close the app completely and reopen it. If the issue continues, make sure you have the latest version installed, then contact us by email with your device model and iOS version.

Pricing

Retro Street Games is free to download and play. It contains advertisements and a single optional in-app purchase that removes ads. There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges.
