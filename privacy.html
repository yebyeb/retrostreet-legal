Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

Thank you for playing Retro Street Games (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Your nickname, game progress, stars, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard platform storage. This data never leaves your device through our own code.

2. Online Multiplayer

When you play an online match, a temporary game room (a room code, the nickname you chose, and in-match progress) is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database solely to synchronize the match between players. This data is not used to identify you, is not sold, and temporary room data expires automatically.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process device information and advertising identifiers. Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies: Google Ads Privacy and Google Privacy Policy. Where required, the App presents a consent (UMP) form before personalized ads are shown.

4. Purchases

The App is free and supported by ads. An optional one-time in-app purchase removes ads. Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

5. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment information. The App has no account or login system.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy

These services operate independently and under their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Temporary online room data expires automatically. We do not maintain server-side user profiles.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately after being posted on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at yebappcontact@gmail.com.
